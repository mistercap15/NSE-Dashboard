# Frozen candidate screen — 14 October 2026

Objective: reject weak candidates and identify evidence worth forward evaluation. This is not a commitment to find or deploy a profitable strategy. Freeze before the new runs; do not select parameters after reading results.

## Candidates (three only)

1. Existing gap-first-pullback V1: advance 0.5%, all existing config.json rules unchanged.
2. Approved gap-first-pullback V2: advance 0.25%, all other rules identical to V1.
3. Gap opening-range breakout (ORB15): same 1–10% gap, prior liquidity/history, five-stock ranking, capital, fees, sizing and exits. Instead of the advance/pullback, collect the complete 09:15, 09:20 and 09:25 bars. At 09:30, require the third close above session VWAP, then arm a buy stop one tick above the three-bar high, initial stop one tick below their low. Earliest execution is 09:30 onward, never the range's own bars. Pending expires after 18 five-minute bars or 11:00, whichever first. One attempt per stock/day. Target 2R and square-off 15:15. No parameter grid.

## Common execution and capital

Unlevered ₹200,000 separate simulated accounts, initial risk including estimated charges <=0.25%, allocation <=25%, <=2 positions, <=3 daily entries, 1% daily loss halt. Reuse the tested engine's conservative stop gaps, entry/stop ambiguity, next-bar eligibility, integer shares, prior-volume participation and cash constraints. No unknown-order same-entry-bar target is credited. Fees use the existing conservative research schedule, not verified account billing. Base 5bp per side includes assumed spread/slippage; stress 10bp per side plus double explicit fees. Observed candles cannot prove executable depth or fill times within intervals.

No claim that 2025–2026 is untouched out-of-sample. All those years have already been inspected. Report all six candidate/cost combinations, years, stock contributions, trade logs, equity, drawdown and profit factor, including losses.

NIFTY futures screening remains blocked by the audited export's missing lot sizes and contract/data anomalies. Crypto is outside this initial NSE cash screen pending market preference.

## Gates before considering live deployment

A historical winner must have positive net expectancy, base PF >=1.2, stress PF >1, positive net results in at least three calendar years, >=100 closed trades, and no one stock contributing more than half of positive aggregate net P&L. These are screening thresholds, not statistical proof. Require a broader dated, corporate-action-validated universe and sensitivity to missing/ambiguous data before calling it robust.

Then freeze rules for at least 60 forward sessions AND 100 completed paper trades (whichever takes longer), positive net expectancy/PF >=1.2 after observed spread and fees, no unresolved execution/ledger faults, and a positive day-block-bootstrap 95% lower bound on mean daily net P&L. Retain every trial and no-trade day. Failure means rejection or a newly documented experiment, not quiet retuning. A future small live trial requires separate user approval, confirmed instruments/account costs, fixed capital/loss limits and verified bot/order controls. Nothing in this screen deploys a trading strategy.
