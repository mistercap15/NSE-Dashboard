# Gap / first pullback V1 — frozen 28 September 2026, before replay

Mechanical adaptation inspired by Ross Cameron; not an exact reproduction or established profitable strategy. The attached user rules are implemented without threshold optimization. config.json is authoritative and SHA-256 hashed into every persisted state and report. No live execution code exists.

Long-only NSE ordinary cash shares. Date-effective eligibility and tick sizes come from an explicit universe manifest; unknown ordinary-share, suspension or tick metadata excludes a stock. Prior 20 valid regular sessions required. Daily traded-value proxy is sum(typical five-minute price × volume), median at least INR 100 million. This is NOT exact exchange traded value or close × daily volume. Current-key pilot is labelled current-survivor sample, never a historical market universe.

Gap uses first valid 09:15 bar open / comparable prior close minus 1; shortlist at 09:20 by gap descending (1%–10%), prior median value descending, symbol ascending, top five. First-bar presence and freshness required, ranking uses no later candles. Previous session must match the supplied calendar. Unknown corporate actions default to quarantine; explicit date-level comparable-close factors are supported. A declared, separate exploratory variant may assume unadjusted continuity, but is not reliable economic-gap evidence. Adjusted-close factor maps prior close and prior EMA into current units; unresolved events reset indicator/liquidity warmup and disable the stock that day.

EMA9 uses closed candles and carries between valid regular sessions, at least 50 prior candles. Session VWAP is cumulative typical-price × volume / cumulative volume; zero-volume does not create a value. Complete regular sessions are used to update the 20-session liquidity history. Calendar rejects unknown dates and explicit special sessions. Missing interior bars invalidate new setups and reset indicator warmup; existing positions still exit using the next observed executable price. No imputation.

Advance close >= open×1.005 by 10:00, at or after selection; only subsequent candles can start a pullback. First close below previous close begins it. Exactly that pullback is tracked; 2–5 consecutive lower closes, most recent close within 0.5% of EMA9 OR session VWAP, every low strictly above session open. Interpret failure at candle 2 as immediate ineligibility if proximity fails. Non-lower close before arming invalidates; after arming it leaves the existing trigger valid until expiry, but cannot form a new pullback. Sixth lower close invalidates after processing prior trigger. No later replacement pullback.

Trigger = latest eligible completed pullback high + valid tick, rounded up. Stop = tracked low − tick, rounded down. Process previously armed order against the next candle BEFORE updating on its close. Updated order keeps original three-bar lifetime (updates do not extend expiry). Entry cutoff is 11:00 exclusive. One entry per stock/day, deterministic shortlist-rank then symbol ordering for simultaneous candidates.

Candle fills: opening gap uses worse open plus adverse slippage; stops gap to worse open minus slippage. Both touched => stop first. An entry within a bar never receives that bar’s target; adverse feasible stop can count. Target needs one tick penetration; favorable gaps earn target only. No trailing/partial exits. Square-off scheduled at 15:15 open; on missing observation use next valid price with delayed flag, including next day if necessary. Such overnight artifacts remain flagged, block new entries and use conservative delivery-tax assumptions at delayed exits. Candle timestamps identify intervals, not exact intrabar event times.

Participation cap: 1% of the PREVIOUS completed candle volume for entries (causal, not future entry-bar volume). Whole shares; projected entry plus exit charges included in cash/allocation budgets and planned risk. Initial INR 200,000, no leverage, 0.25% planned risk, 25% allocation, two positions, aggregate risk <=0.50%, three daily entries. Daily loss halt at 1% marked equity; sampled at each bar close and conservatively at opening marks before entries; exits remain active. Portfolio equity uses latest observed close, with stale marks flagged. Planned risk cannot guarantee actual gap losses.

Candle mode slippage 5bp each side, stressed at 10bp and double itemized charges; spread is not independently measured. Baseline fee schedule is an explicit conservative constant assumption, NOT historically exact: brokerage min(0.1%, INR20)/side; exchange 0.00345%/side; SEBI 0.0001%; IPFT 0.0001%; GST18% on brokerage/exchange/SEBI/IPFT; stamp0.003% buy; STT0.025% sell. Rounded half-up per component to paise; contract-note rounding may differ. Fee schedules may be overridden by dated config before a new experiment. No fixed index-point charges.

Quote execution adapter uses ask/bid, quote receipt and source timestamps, 5-second age limit, no crossed/zero quote, and flags insufficient depth; slippage remains separate, spread is not added again. Quote mode requires sequential quote events and closed-candle signal events; candle callbacks do not synthesize quote fills. Recorded quote receipt ordering determines causality. No claim of full depth fill certainty. Default observer stays candle-modeled.

Pause blocks entries only. Reset creates a new paper account ID and archives prior SQLite account row; duplicate events cannot repeat cashflows. Any config/manifest/calendar change requires a new account. No Telegram delivery, bot imports, service changes, deployment or persistent activation during implementation.

Predeclared sensitivity variants (before this replay): strict = require reviewed comparable-close factors; continuity-assumption = unverified factor 1 on missing reviews; stress = continuity with 10bp each side and 2× fees; exclude-ambiguous = continuity rejecting intrabar entries whose candle also reaches their stop; complete-only = continuity excluding every session with any missing pilot-stock candle and rebuilding warmup. These are sensitivity checks, not optimized alternatives. Ambiguity exclusion is an ex-post diagnostic conditioned on the observed candle range, not a causal rule suitable for trading. Intrabar exits cannot release capital for other entries within that same interval; known opening stop gaps and scheduled opening square-offs can.

Forward polling records actual receipt separately. A trigger learned partway through an interval is actionable only from the next whole interval; it cannot use an earlier high from the partially elapsed interval. Historical replay assumes the prior closed signal is actionable at its close. Poll batches more than 90 seconds late pause new entries until explicitly resumed. The candle polling adapter supports at most 60 reviewed symbols; a market-wide forward stream adapter has not been activated or claimed. Offline replay/downloading supports broader manifests.
