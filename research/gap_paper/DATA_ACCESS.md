# Architecture inspection and data access — 28 September 2026

Inspected existing `app/lib/upstox.js`, `app/lib/instrumentMaster.js`, `scripts/fetch_nifty_fut_oi.py`, `scripts/probe_cash_equity_coverage.py`, the dashboard/API layout, PIN middleware, Telegram integration patterns, and existing research exports. Existing chart route primarily supplies daily history; no suitable paper database or certified historical equity universe/calendar/action feed was found. Avoided importing executor/OAuth synchronization code. SQLite uses the Python standard library; no service or bot was changed. Telegram support is a formatting function only, without a delivery transport.

Official sources consulted:

- [Analytics token](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/analytics-token/): read-only historical, market-quote and websocket categories; no trading operations. Local authenticated `GET /v3/market-quote/quotes` succeeded for RELIANCE and returned buy/sell depth. Sanitized probe saved in `data/exports/gap_paper/capabilities/quote_probe.json`. No subscription/stream was started, and entitlement to every websocket mode was not empirically tested.
- [Full quotes V3](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-full-market-quote-v3/): bid/ask depth and quote timestamps are available. Historical candle files do not contain historical bid/ask; therefore spread is assumed through adverse fill slippage, not measured. No separate spread subtraction.
- [Historical candles V3](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-historical-candle-data/): minute data documented from January 2022; five-minute retrieval limited to one month per request. The cached five-stock data actually ends 25 September 2026. Empty 2021 responses do not establish coverage.
- [Rate limits](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/rate-limiting/): standard APIs 50/sec, 500/min, 2,000/30min. This client uses one request/sec and bounded backoff; limits are shared with other applications. The capped polling adapter is intentionally small; no claim of a market-wide low-latency feed.
- [Instrument files](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/instruments/): current keys, lot and tick metadata, and separate suspended-instrument list. Current files do not reconstruct delisted/historical membership. Candidate generator retains a proposed tick conversion from provider JSON paise to rupees; verify it against dated exchange/broker metadata before eligibility. Historical pilot assumes ₹0.05, which is not a verified historical tick schedule. Candidates remain disabled; share type and suspension need review.
- [Brokerage schedule](https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/), [account opening form](https://assets.upstox.com/website/forms/individual-account-opening-form.pdf), [STT description](https://upstox.com/glossary/what-is-the-securities-transaction-tax-stt/), [October 2024 charge revision](https://upstox.com/announcements/updates/revision-in-transaction-charges-from-1-october-2024/): intraday equity brokerage/tax basis and historical schedule changes. No account-specific contract-note schedule was obtained. V1 uses the explicit conservative flat rates in `config.json`, supports dated overrides, and reports a doubled-charge sensitivity. These are not asserted as exact fees throughout 2022–2026. Components use half-up paise rounding; statutory/contract-note aggregation may differ. Delayed overnight exit cases are flagged and use delivery sell-STT, but full delivery conversion/DP charges are not modeled; none occurred in this pilot.

Historical findings and limitations:

- RELIANCE, HDFCBANK, INFY, ITC, TATASTEEL only. Current surviving keys, not a market-wide or historically unbiased universe.
- Additional invalid input: HDFCBANK 25 June 2024 09:15 has O=835.6, H=843.8, L=837.4, C=843.8 (low exceeds open); rejected, never repaired. This reduces valid rows from 87,689 to 87,688 and creates a second incomplete HDFCBANK session.
- Common missing 7 March 2022 09:30 candle persists in the earlier separate API recheck. No interpolation.
- RELIANCE 20 July 2023 starts at 10:00 (demerger-related session difference); ITC 6 January 2025 starts at 09:35. These are incomplete regular sessions and excluded from setups/warmup continuity. Corporate-action comparability is not inferred from a price jump.
- Known evening Muhurat and Saturday special sessions are excluded. Calendar derives from observed daily bars with explicit exceptions; a fully missing shared regular session can evade detection until an authoritative exchange calendar is supplied.
- Nonnegative integer candle volume is available, including zero-volume bars. Earlier daily-vs-five-minute volume reconciliation differs (median absolute discrepancy approximately 0.34–0.81% by stock); no tick-level certification. Liquidity uses the documented typical-price×volume proxy.
- Detailed per-symbol dates, empty responses, missing bar lists, zero-volume counts, raw discontinuities, file hashes and exclusions are exported beside every replay.

Strict action review intentionally blocks performance claims. A broader historical study additionally needs dated ordinary-share membership, key changes/delistings, suspension history, tick schedules, comparable-close/action evidence and a complete exchange calendar. The implementation accepts these inputs; it does not invent them. Forward observations remain the appropriate next independent evaluation once the reviewed inputs exist.
